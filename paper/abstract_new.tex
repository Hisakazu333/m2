Representation repair changes task loss and the evidence available for later decisions, so any account of repair must state which label a later answer is required to certify. This paper studies two evaluation contracts inside one joint observation--state model with explicit loss accounting: fixed-confidence certification of the old representation's label, and finite-horizon Bayes control with a terminal declaration about the current representation's label. The two contracts share the intervention viewpoint and the same physical kernels, but their objective functions differ, and policies are compared only within one contract at a time. For the old-label contract, in an ordered single-channel experiment with observable environment-independent repair failures and free post-repair observations, certification reduces to choosing a decoder on post-repair observational classes, with an exact leading cost coefficient $g_i\log(1/\delta)/D_i$, a common attaining policy, and fully charged deadline tails (Theorem~\ref{thm:main}). A classwise factorization computes the coefficient, and a polynomial eligibility test implements the same likelihood-ratio race without exponential decoder enumeration (Theorems~\ref{thm:factorization} and \ref{thm:polynomial}). A finite-deadline information lower bound is matched by a shrinking-channel regime in which distinct post laws fail to improve the leading coefficient (Theorems~\ref{thm:deadline-lower} and \ref{thm:collapse}). For the current-label contract, we give an exact declaration-gate cost identity through Bellman advantages, bound the number of repair-region components by the affine pieces of passive post-repair decision risk, and exhibit a full-support TP2 example with two disconnected repair regions and strict intervention below the declaration boundary (Theorems~\ref{thm:gate}, \ref{thm:geometry}, and \ref{thm:counter}). The analysis uses established sequential-testing and POMDP tools; the stated contracts and structural assumptions delimit every claim, and an executable implementation with exact rational Bayes computation and independent policy-tree enumeration accompanies the results.
